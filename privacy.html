Privacy Statement

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Statement explains how Solar Client ("Solar Client", "we", "us", or "our") may collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit our website or use Solar Client.

We aim to collect only information that is necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and improve our services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on which features you use, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Basic account information.
  • Authentication information.
  • Technical information about your device.
  • Application and error information.
  • Information you voluntarily provide to us.

We do not intentionally collect information that is unnecessary for operating Solar Client.

3. Microsoft Authentication

Solar Client may provide Microsoft account authentication for supported features.

Authentication is handled through Microsoft's authentication systems. Solar Client does not request or store your Microsoft account password.

When authentication is used, we may receive limited account information and authentication tokens required to provide the requested functionality.

Microsoft services are also subject to Microsoft's own privacy policies and terms.

4. Authentication Tokens

Authentication tokens may be used to keep you signed in and to access services you have requested.

Access tokens are generally temporary. Where supported, refresh mechanisms may be used to obtain new access credentials without requiring you to repeatedly sign in.

Tokens should be treated as sensitive authentication information and should not be shared with other users.

5. Device and Technical Information

When you use Solar Client, technical information may be processed to operate and secure the application.

This may include information such as operating system, application version, crash information, and technical diagnostics.

We do not use technical information to identify you personally unless this is necessary for a specific service or required by law.

6. Website Information

When visiting our website, standard technical information may be processed by the hosting provider.

Depending on the hosting configuration, this can include IP addresses, browser information, timestamps, and requested pages.

The website may also use essential technologies required for security and functionality.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

Solar Client may use browser storage technologies such as local storage or cookies where necessary for website functionality.

These technologies may be used to remember preferences, maintain sessions, or provide requested functionality.

If optional analytics or advertising technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Statement will be updated accordingly.

8. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing Solar Client features.
  • Authenticating users.
  • Maintaining account sessions.
  • Protecting our services from abuse.
  • Diagnosing technical problems.
  • Improving application stability.
  • Responding to support requests.
  • Complying with legal obligations.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate Solar Client, such as hosting, authentication, infrastructure, or security providers.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security and rights of users or our services.

10. Third-Party Services

Solar Client may use third-party services such as Microsoft authentication, hosting providers, download infrastructure, or other technical services.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

You should review the privacy policies of third-party services that you choose to use.

11. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

However, no internet service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Statement, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Authentication credentials and temporary tokens may expire automatically according to the relevant authentication provider's policies.

13. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information.

These may include rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or information about how your data is processed.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the official contact information provided on the Solar Client website.

14. Children's Privacy

Solar Client is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us.

15. International Processing

Depending on the services and infrastructure used by Solar Client, information may be processed in countries other than the country in which you live.

Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international data transfers.

16. Changes to This Privacy Statement

We may update this Privacy Statement when our services, technology, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be published on this page.

17. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Statement or how Solar Client processes information, please contact the Solar Client team through the official contact channel provided on our website.
